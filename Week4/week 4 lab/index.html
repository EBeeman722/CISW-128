Welcome One and All

Arcanopunk is a magepunk co-op heisting fps made by your favourite indie developer, me!

As said in the title, Arcanopunk is four player heisting game set in an environment where magic takes precident and contributed greatly to the development of humanity in place of technology

You'll play as one of eight currently planned out characters, themed after reverse tarot cards, aiming to send a company that had a hand in the heisting group's leader's downfall into a financial downspiral.

Some major inspirations for this project include, but are not limited to, the following games:

  • Payday 2/3
  • Cyberpunk 2077
  • Wizard with a Gun
